Testing was automated from the start. 63 automated tests run with pytest and all pass: 40 test the API and business rules, 13 are end-to-end tests that walk a customer from survey to warranty, and 10 test creating and deleting records safely. On top of that, every screen was checked in the browser for each role, on a copy of the database. The right side lists real bugs testing caught: GST was still 12 percent after the September 2025 cut; the survey accepted an impossible 4,545 kilowatts on a 40 square foot roof; entries were lost when pressing Next; a shared panel batch left one order without a warranty; an installation could be finished before its scheduled day; and the dispatch screen repeated information. Each one is now fixed and covered by a test.
