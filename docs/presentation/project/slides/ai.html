Installation job

Runs on free local models in a separate service. AI only fills in: staff check every result, and everything can still be typed by hand.

Four AI helpers reduce typing. On the savings page a customer can upload a photo of their electricity bill, and the bill reader pulls out units, amount and consumer number and suggests a system size. In a new enquiry, sales can paste a WhatsApp message and the message reader fills in the name, phone, city and requirement. On a service request, fault diagnosis suggests the likely cause, safe checks and parts. And when a technician uploads an installation photo, the photo checker labels the panels clean, dusty, bird-dropped or damaged, with a confidence score. The AI runs locally on free models, it only fills in fields, and staff always check the result.
