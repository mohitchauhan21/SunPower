This is the whole flow in twelve steps. The customer books a survey. Sales assigns a technician using the availability grid. The technician surveys the roof and builds the quote, which goes straight to the customer. The customer accepts. Sales schedules the installation. The warehouse packs the kit and dispatches it, recording every serial number. The crew installs and tests. Finishing the job starts the warranties and issues the final invoice. Later, service handles repairs, and the director sees it all on the dashboard. The orange notes show what the system does automatically, and the rules it enforces, for example no dispatch without stock and every serial number.
