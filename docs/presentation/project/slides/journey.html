Let’s follow one customer, Aarav Nair from Ahmedabad, who wants a 3 kilowatt on-grid system. He checks his savings on our website and books a survey, creating an account on the way. His enquiry gets a project ID and appears on the sales team’s Today page. Sales books technician Ramesh using the availability grid. Ramesh does the survey on his phone, and building the quote sends it to Aarav automatically. Aarav accepts online. Sales schedules the installation, the warehouse dispatches the kit with every serial recorded, Ramesh installs it, and Aarav’s warranties, GST invoice and installation certificate appear in his portal. This is exactly what the live demo will show.
