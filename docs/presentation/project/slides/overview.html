In one line: SunPower is one connected system for a rooftop solar company. Instead of separate spreadsheets, WhatsApp chats and paper registers, every step lives in one place: enquiries, roof surveys, quotations, the warehouse, installation, warranties, repairs, government subsidy files and the director’s reports. Because every module is connected, information entered once flows to the next step automatically. AI helps with typing, and management sees the business live.
