Architecture in three layers. At the top, the users: the public website where customers book, the customer portal, and one staff workspace that adapts to six roles, with technician pages that work on a phone. The front end is a React single-page app built with Vite. It talks to a FastAPI back end in Python, where all the business rules live, together with role-based access and sign-in. Data is stored in SQLite. On the side runs a separate AI helper service with free local models, and the system connects to PVGIS for solar estimates, Razorpay for payments, Google sign-in and email.
