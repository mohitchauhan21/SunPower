Our answer is one platform shared by seven roles: the customer, sales, the survey technician, the warehouse, the installation crew, the service desk and the director. Every customer project gets one universal ID, like SP-26-00058, that appears on the survey, the quote, the dispatch, the installation, the invoices and the warranties. Information is entered once: what the technician measures on the roof becomes lines on the quotation and items on the warehouse packing list. And access is role-based, so a technician only sees their own jobs.
