Here are the nine modules. Enquiries and booking, with a technician availability grid. Roof surveys with an eight-section form. Quotes and orders, with packages from 1 to 100 kilowatts, site-specific extras, GST and payment plans. Inventory and dispatch, with unique item codes and serial scanning. Installations with checklist, photo and signature. The warranty register, which tracks every unit by serial. Service requests. The subsidy tracker for the government PM Surya Ghar scheme. And the director’s dashboard. Customers also get their own portal with invoices, the installation certificate and their warranties.
