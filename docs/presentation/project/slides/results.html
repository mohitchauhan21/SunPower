Results, limitations & future scope

Where the project stands

Achieved

  • Survey-to-warranty workflow
  • 9 modules plus customer portal
  • 4 AI helpers
  • 63 / 63 automated tests
  • One project ID on every record
  • Demo data: 52 customers at every stage

Limitations

  • Runs locally, not yet deployed

Future scope

  • Cloud deployment with backups
  • WhatsApp Business API, SMS
  • Technician mobile app
  • Supplier purchase orders

To sum up results: the complete workflow from survey to warranty works, with nine modules plus a customer portal, four AI helpers, all 63 automated tests passing, one project ID on every record, and realistic demo data with 52 customers at every stage. The honest limitations: it runs locally and isn’t deployed yet, the payment webhook needs a public address and emails need an SMTP account, local AI can make mistakes so staff verify it, and there’s no WhatsApp integration or native mobile app yet. Those are exactly the next steps: cloud deployment, WhatsApp and SMS, a technician app, supplier purchase orders, switching systems on after full payment, and forecasting.
