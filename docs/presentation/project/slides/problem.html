Problem statement

A small solar company runs on WhatsApp, paper and memory

Enquiries scattered across WhatsApp, calls and the website

Quotations typed by hand, one at a time

Serial numbers written on paper

No clear picture of stock or shortages

Warranty details lost when a fault comes in

Site photos stuck on technicians’ phones

No idea which jobs made money

Follow-ups forgotten, customers left waiting

The result

Slow quotes, lost details and no way to track a customer from enquiry to warranty.

Each team keeps its own copy of the truth, and nothing connects them.

This is how many small solar installers work today. Enquiries come in on WhatsApp, phone and the website and nobody has one list. Quotes are typed by hand. Serial numbers of panels and inverters go into a notebook, so when a fault comes in a year later nobody can find the warranty. Photos of the installation stay on a technician’s phone. And the owner can’t tell which jobs made a profit. The common thread: every team has its own copy of the truth.
