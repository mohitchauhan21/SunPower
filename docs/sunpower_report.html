1. The short version

SunPower installs about 40 rooftop solar systems a month, has 15 technicians and keeps expensive stock (panels, inverters, batteries). Today enquiries get lost in WhatsApp, quotes are typed by hand, serial numbers sit on paper, and the owner cannot see which jobs made money.

We built one connected system where each step creates the next one automatically: an enquiry becomes a survey, an approved survey allows a quote, a confirmed order tells the warehouse and the crew, a delivery starts every warranty, and every rupee and hour lands on the job so the director can see profit per installation.

Why Odoo? Odoo is free business software that already does customers, quotes, stock, invoices, staff and websites. We added only the solar-specific parts on top. That is the difference between finishing in 24 hours and spending 24 hours rebuilding an invoice screen.

5. One customer's journey

This is the exact path tested in Phase 2 with a sample customer, Meera Joshi, who wants a 3 kW system.

  1. Website. Meera fills the "Get a Solar Quote" form: 3 kW On-Grid, ₹3,200 monthly bill, 420 sq ft roof, wants subsidy. She sees "Thank you, reference 48".
  2. Sales board. Enquiry 48 appears, tagged Website Form and No Survey, with a "call within 2 hours" reminder. The system suggests 3 kW from the bill.
  3. Phone check. Trying to book a survey before confirming the grid connection is refused with a clear message. After marking the grid "Suitable", the survey is created and pre-filled. It works out that up to 4.2 kW fits.
  4. Survey. Marking it "visited" without the roof direction is refused. After entering South, 15° tilt and 18 m of cable, it is approved.
  5. Quote. Before approval the quote is blocked. After approval, "New Quotation" fills the 3 kW On-Grid package: ₹1,44,000 kit + ₹15,000 installation = ₹1,59,000, linked to the survey.
  6. Next phases: 30% advance → warehouse packs and scans → warranties start → crew installs and Meera signs → certificate emailed → subsidy file moves forward → profit shows on the dashboard.

6. What happens on its own

These are the rules that remove phone calls and forgotten steps. Staff do not have to remember any of them.

When this happens……the system does this
An enquiry arrives from any channelAssigns the least-busy salesperson and adds a "call within 2 hours" reminder
Someone tries to book a survey earlyRefuses until roof area, bill and grid connection are filled
Someone tries to quote earlyRefuses to create, send or confirm a quote until the survey is approved
A quote is startedPicks the package matching the survey's kW and system type
The order is confirmedCreates the warehouse packing list, books the installation job, opens a subsidy file
The truck is about to leaveRequires every inverter, battery and controller serial; blocks dispatch until the minimum advance (30%) is paid
The delivery is marked doneCreates a warranty for every serial with the correct end date
The crew taps "Finish"Refuses without checklist, photos, kW and signature; logs hours; emails the certificate
A repair is finishedFree if under warranty; otherwise creates and posts an invoice; sends the customer a confirm link
Every hourRecalculates profit for every job
How job profit is worked out: contract value (before tax) − parts delivered (at cost) − technician hours × hourly rate − free warranty repairs. A job below zero shows in red.

7. The AI helpers

Four helpers save typing. They never save anything on their own: they fill boxes, a person checks and saves. Because there are no paid credits, all four run with free models on the laptop, so customer data never leaves it.

WhatsApp / voice readerPasted message or voice note (Hindi/Hinglish ok)Name, phone, area, kW, subsidy, enquiry or complaintWhisper (speech to text) + small language modelSwitching to free models
Fault-code helperFault code + descriptionLikely cause, safe steps, visit needed, partsSearches a written guide of ~30 inverter faults, then writes advice only from itWorking (guide)
Panel photo checkPhoto of panelsClean / dusty / bird-drop / damaged, with confidenceImage model trained on solar panel photos (ConvNeXt)Working

The Odoo buttons that call these helpers ("Read bill", "Read message", "AI diagnose", "Check photos") are built in Phase 8, together with a test that switches the AI off and confirms nothing else breaks.

12. Risks and suggestions

Risks

  • Disk space is low: about 12 GB free. AI model downloads use 9.7 GB; keeping only the models in use frees several GB.
  • Project folder is inside OneDrive: the AI helper's 2 GB+ program files try to sync to the cloud. Moving them next to Odoo avoids that.
  • Free AI models are slower and less accurate, especially the bill reader. Staff correct the boxes; nothing is saved without a person.
  • Real WhatsApp connection is not included: it needs a Meta-approved business account, which takes days. Staff paste messages or upload voice notes instead.
  • The Hugging Face token was shared in chat: generate a new one after the hackathon.

Suggestions

  • Faster testing: keep the browser panel visible, and run the server in developer mode so most fixes need only a page refresh instead of a restart.
  • Practise one story for the judges: follow Meera through all six scenes in about 7 minutes.
  • Turn off developer mode for the demo and run rebuild_db.py once beforehand for clean data.
  • After the hackathon: real WhatsApp integration, SMS reminders, and running on a proper server so the team can use it from anywhere.
